Project Overview

AgriConnect Rwanda is a digital marketplace designed to help smallholder farmers. The platform allows farmers to access market prices, connect with buyers, organize transport and reduce post-harvest losses.

Core Solutions

  • Marketplace: Farmers can sell products online.
  • Transport Integration: Transporters provide delivery services.
  • Weather Analysis: Farmers receive weather updates.
  • Efficiency: Better coordination reduces waste.
